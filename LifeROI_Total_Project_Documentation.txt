1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
====================

LifeROI is an interactive resource-intelligence application that helps a user
understand the long-term impact of saving three important life resources:

  1. Money
  2. Time
  3. Energy

The central product question is:

  "What could my life become if I saved a little more?"

The application converts small daily improvements into understandable future
outcomes. Examples include money redirected toward a financial goal, time
recovered for learning or fitness, and electricity savings redirected toward
more efficient living.

The current public application is a polished synthetic-data prototype. It is
appropriate for concept demonstrations, interaction testing, and explaining the
LifeROI vision. It is not yet a production system for real financial or personal
documents.

Important current boundary:

  - A selected file is checked for name, size, extension, and MIME type.
  - The application does not read, extract, upload, or store its contents.
  - Dashboard values are generated synthetic values, not document-derived data.
  - D1 and R2 bindings and a database schema exist, but application CRUD APIs,
    real persistence, and private document processing are not implemented.


2. PROJECT VISION
=================

LifeROI is based on the idea that return on investment should not be measured
only in money. A better life can also be created by recovering attention,
protecting energy, improving health, learning, and increasing freedom of choice.

The product aims to help a user:

  - See where money, time, and energy are currently going.
  - Discover low-disruption opportunities to save each resource.
  - Change assumptions and immediately see the future effect.
  - Compare the current path with an optimized path.
  - Connect savings to meaningful goals and investments.
  - Receive encouraging, data-grounded guidance.
  - Build consistency through motivational design and positive feedback.


3. TARGET USERS
===============

Primary users:

  - Individuals who want a combined view of personal spending, time use, and
    household energy consumption.
  - People working toward savings, fitness, learning, sustainability, or
    lifestyle goals.
  - Users who find traditional financial dashboards too narrow or static.

Secondary audiences:

  - Product-demo reviewers and academic evaluators.
  - Personal-finance and wellbeing educators.
  - Future partners providing financial, screen-time, or utility data.


4. CURRENT PRODUCT FEATURES
===========================

4.1 Overview
------------

The Overview is the main story of the application. It combines available
resource data and displays:

  - A rotating expert-wisdom section.
  - Money, time, and energy indicators.
  - Cross-resource opportunities.
  - A goal-progress view.
  - Time and energy opportunity summaries.
  - Resource-saving projections.
  - Data confidence and source labels.

Dashboard information is designed to appear only when the corresponding data
is available.

4.2 Resource Worlds
-------------------

Resources are organized under one parent area with three distinct views:

  - Money: spending categories, recurring costs, savings opportunities, and
    financial projections.
  - Time: daily activity allocation, screen-time breakdown, weekly rhythm, and
    reclaimed-time possibilities.
  - Energy: electricity use, cost, efficiency opportunity, and an energy-saver
    plan.

4.3 Future Canvas
-----------------

Future Canvas provides separate Money, Time, and Energy projection modes.
The user can change:

  - Savings or reduction percentage.
  - Time horizon from 1 to 30 years.
  - Illustrative annual return for the money scenario.

The output changes immediately and presents the result in encouraging language.
The application clearly separates arithmetic cash savings from illustrative
investment growth.

4.4 Investment
--------------

The Investment section recommends constructive destinations for saved resources.

Money examples:

  - Emergency reserves.
  - Goal-based saving.
  - Diversified, educational investment options.

Time examples:

  - Reading books such as Atomic Habits, The Psychology of Money, and Deep Work.
  - Fitness and strength-building.
  - Rest, creativity, and personal relationships.

Energy examples:

  - High-efficiency appliance reserves.
  - Smart home efficiency improvements.
  - Solar-readiness or sustainability projects.

These recommendations are educational suggestions, not personalized financial
or medical advice.

4.5 Goals
---------

The goal module compares:

  - Current savings.
  - Target amount.
  - Current monthly contribution.
  - Estimated time on the current path.
  - Estimated time after the selected optimization.

The target can be changed interactively, and the timeline is recalculated.

4.6 Upload Workspace
--------------------

The Upload workspace accepts the selection of these demo formats:

  - PDF
  - CSV
  - TXT
  - PNG
  - JPG/JPEG

Maximum demo file size: 10 MB.

Current upload behavior:

  - Validates file metadata.
  - Rejects empty, oversized, unsupported, or MIME-mismatched files.
  - Generates a new synthetic cross-resource dataset after validation.
  - Updates Money, Time, Energy, Subscriptions, Goals, Opportunities,
    Future Canvas, Investment, and LifeROI Compass from that dataset.

No file body is read or stored in the current public prototype.

4.7 LifeROI Compass
-------------------

LifeROI Compass is the interactive advisory dialog. It supports:

  - Suggested questions that populate the input field.
  - Keyboard submission using Enter.
  - Questions about overspending, goals, recurring costs, money, time, and
    energy.
  - Answers derived from the currently active synthetic dataset.
  - Focus management, Escape-to-close, and keyboard-safe modal behavior.

The current advisor uses deterministic rules and prepared responses. It is not
connected to an external large-language-model API.

4.8 Settings and Information Dialogs
------------------------------------

Settings includes interactive retention choices:

  - Delete source after extraction.
  - Keep source for 30 days.
  - Keep source until manually deleted.

In the current prototype, these controls demonstrate the intended user
experience. Real deletion and retention jobs require the production backend.

Information buttons explain the Resource Score, projections, energy, time, and
privacy behavior.

4.9 Engagement and Motivation
-----------------------------

The interface contains:

  - Animated background decorations.
  - Emoji celebrations when inputs change.
  - Encouraging projection messages.
  - Rotating expert quotes with source links.
  - A personalized demo character based on name and gender.
  - A tortoise-and-rabbit progress race illustrating that slow and steady
    consistency wins.
  - Reduced-motion support for users who prefer less animation.

9. PLANNED PRODUCTION SEQUENCE
==============================

  1. User signs in.
  2. Worker reads the stable user identity from trusted platform headers.
  3. User uploads a supported document.
  4. Server verifies file signature, type, size, page/decompression limits, and
     idempotency.
  5. Source document is stored using an opaque, owner-scoped R2 key.
  6. AI classifies the document and returns a strictly structured payload.
  7. Server validates every field, confidence value, unit, date, and currency.
  8. The Review and Confirm screen shows extracted values and provenance.
  9. Only confirmed data is written to owner-scoped D1 records.
 10. Domain services calculate opportunities, projections, and goal timelines.
 11. Dashboard and advisor retrieve only the signed-in user's confirmed data.
 12. Retention, export, source deletion, and account deletion are enforced by
     server-side workflows.

11. DOMAIN CALCULATIONS
=======================

All core arithmetic is deterministic and implemented outside any AI model.

11.1 Money projection
---------------------

Monthly savings:

  monthly_savings = monthly_spending * (reduction_percentage / 100)

Annual savings:

  annual_savings = monthly_savings * 12

Cash value after N months:

  future_cash = current_savings + (monthly_savings * months)

Investment scenario using an ordinary monthly annuity:

  monthly_rate = annual_return / 100 / 12

  future_investment =
      current_savings * (1 + monthly_rate)^months
      + monthly_savings * (((1 + monthly_rate)^months - 1) / monthly_rate)

If the return is zero, the investment value equals the cash value.

Inflation-adjusted scenario:

  real_value = future_investment / (1 + inflation_rate)^years

11.2 Time recovery
------------------

  daily_minutes_recovered = minutes_per_day * reduction_percentage / 100
  weekly_hours_recovered   = daily_minutes_recovered * 7 / 60
  annual_hours_recovered   = daily_minutes_recovered * 365 / 60

11.3 Goal timing
----------------

  months_to_goal = ceiling((target - current) / monthly_contribution)

Special cases:

  - If current value already meets the target, result is zero months.
  - If contribution is zero while the goal is incomplete, the result is
    effectively infinite.
  - Invalid, negative, NaN, infinite, or out-of-range inputs are rejected.

16. SECURITY AND PRIVACY MODEL
==============================

Current prototype protections:

  - Explicit synthetic-data-only notice.
  - File size, extension, MIME type, and empty-file checks.
  - No reading or storage of selected file contents.
  - Deterministic arithmetic input validation.
  - React output escaping for displayed text.
  - Keyboard-safe dialogs and focus restoration.

Required production protections:

  - Authentication and owner authorization on every read and write.
  - Private R2 objects with opaque owner-scoped keys.
  - Short-lived signed downloads.
  - Server-side byte-signature and decompression validation.
  - Malware and password-protected-document policy.
  - Duplicate and overlapping-statement detection.
  - Strict AI response schemas and confidence thresholds.
  - Prompt-injection isolation for untrusted document content.
  - Mandatory Review and Confirm before analytics.
  - Complete export, retention, source deletion, and account deletion.
  - Logs that exclude document bodies and sensitive extracted content.
  - Two-user adversarial tests against cross-user access.

25. GLOSSARY
============

Resource intelligence
  The combined interpretation of money, time, energy, and related life choices.

Life ROI
  The positive future value produced by a present-day resource decision.

Deterministic calculation
  A formula that produces the same result from the same inputs without AI
  interpretation.

Synthetic data
  Artificially generated demo information that does not represent a real user.

Provenance
  Information describing where a value came from and whether it was extracted,
  inferred, or confirmed by the user.

D1
  Cloudflare's SQL database service, used in the planned architecture for
  structured owner-scoped records.

R2
  Cloudflare object storage, planned for private source documents.

Review and Confirm
  The required user step that checks extracted information before it influences
  analytics.

LifeROI demonstrates a broader way to think about personal progress. The current
prototype successfully provides an engaging, responsive, and data-reactive
frontend with deterministic projections and cross-resource recommendations. Its
production future depends on implementing authenticated APIs, private storage,
validated extraction, user confirmation, and complete privacy controls.

The correct demo message is therefore:

  "The interactive LifeROI experience is working today with safe synthetic
  data. The secure personal-data backend is the next production phase."

END OF DOCUMENT
